## chain | 5. Calibration improved prudence, but not broader restraint

{tb_chain}

<div class="table-brief-reading"><b>Takeaway.</b> Calibration improves factual
self-assessment and reduced overconfident bad decisions. It did not improve
evidence updating or goal revision, and it removed only 2% of hidden shortcuts.
A plural-ends reminder was more promising, reducing shortcuts from 6.8% to
1.0% across all 16 tested checkpoints.</div>

<div class="literature-compare">
<div class="literature-compare-title"><i class="lit-tag adjacent">Adjacent precedent</i> From calibrated confidence to aligned behavior</div>
<dl>
<div><dt>What prior work did</dt><dd>The <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.08219">Off-Switch Game</a> models uncertainty about an agent's objective. <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.01928">KnowNo</a> turns calibrated uncertainty into asking for help in robot planning. Other work tests <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.03120">confidence and evidence updating</a> or defines <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.05070">pluralistic alignment</a>.</dd></div>
<div><dt>What it found</dt><dd>Objective uncertainty can create incentives to accept human correction in a formal model. Calibrated runtime uncertainty can improve deferral. Separately, models can be both stubborn about initial answers and too sensitive to criticism. None of these findings shows that factual confidence training creates broad alignment.</dd></div>
<div><dt>What it called for</dt><dd>Systems that preserve uncertainty about objectives, ask for help when needed, update rationally on evidence, and represent multiple human values through multi-objective or pluralistic evaluations.</dd></div>
<div class="project"><dt>What this project adds</dt><dd><b>A matched test of several links in the proposed chain.</b> Confidence training sharply reduced overconfident bad decisions, but did not improve evidence updating or goal revision and barely changed hidden shortcuts. No direct matched study of this full chain was found in this review through August 24, 2026.</dd></div>
<div class="limit"><dt>What it still cannot show</dt><dd>The shortcut scenarios are short and transparent. They are not evidence of scheming. <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.15740">SHADE-Arena</a> is a stronger model: long tasks, tools, hidden harmful objectives, and monitoring.</dd></div>
